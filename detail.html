COFFEE & SLOW LIVING

Coffee, with a little time to yourself.

A warm cup, a quiet seat and a few unhurried minutes. An invitation to make a small pause part of an ordinary day.

AI-generated image. OpenAI

A break without another task

A coffee break can easily become another part of a busy routine: order, answer a message and move on. But it can also be a small piece of time that does not need to produce anything.

Imagine setting your cup on a clear table and sitting down before deciding what comes next. You might read a few pages, watch the light through a window or simply enjoy the drink while it is warm. The moment does not have to be special to be worth keeping.

Choose what you actually enjoy

You do not need an elaborate order or an expensive setup. Choose a drink you like and a place where you feel comfortable. The point of this little ritual is to make space for your own preferences.

Try putting one thing beside your cup: a book, a notebook or nothing at all. Leave the rest of the table clear. A shorter list of things to do can make the break feel like a break.

Something sweet

If you enjoy something sweet with your coffee, choose a favourite rather than searching for a perfect pairing. A pastry, a biscuit or a slice of cake can be part of the moment simply because you like it.

AI-generated image. OpenAI

Notice the contrast between a crisp edge and a soft centre, or the colours on the plate beside your cup. These ordinary details give you something to pay attention to beyond a screen.

A quiet corner

A quiet corner does not need to be a carefully styled room. It might be one end of a kitchen table, a chair beside a window or a comfortable seat in a favourite cafe.

AI-generated image. OpenAI

At home, clear enough room for your cup and whatever you want to bring with you. A plant or a favourite object can make the space feel personal. Comfort matters more than making the corner look like a photograph.
